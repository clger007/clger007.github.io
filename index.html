About Me

I am a machine learning and natural language processing researcher focused on clinical and population health applications. My recent work deploys LLM- and BERT-based pipelines on real-world narrative EMRs to detect hospital-acquired conditions, extract cancer outcomes from pathology, and quantify symptom burden for service planning. I build end-to-end systems—from data governance and labeling workflows to model development, evaluation, and integration—aimed at improving patient care and operational decision-making.

Featured Projects

  • Cancer Complexity Scoring for Workload Planning (2025) — NLP pipeline on 2M+ EMR notes to extract symptom burden (anxiety, depression, pain) with expert-in-the-loop review.
  • Dynamic Detection of Hospital Adverse Events (2024) — LLM-based system to identify AEs from unstructured notes; fine-tuned classifiers exceeded ICD-code baselines.
  • Inpatient Fall Detection from Narrative EMRs using domain-specific BERT (2023) — Alberta EMR–pretrained BERT; improved safety surveillance vs. ICD baselines.
  • Automated Pathology Report Interpretation for Breast Cancer (2023) — Extracted pathologic complete response (pCR) at population scale using rule-based and LLM approaches.
